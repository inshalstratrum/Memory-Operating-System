# Nasl Khalq Seed

Nasl/Khalq is the LifeOS web: the horizontal axis of rights, mercy, service, and relationship. It governs family responsibilities, community contribution, professional communication, client psychology, remote team leadership, huquq, and ihsan.

The system must protect context boundaries between personal relationships, family matters, community responsibilities, and client work. Client psychology parsing should improve helpfulness and tone without manipulation or arrogance.

Relationship success is measured by fulfilling rights with excellence, warmth, trustworthiness, and appropriate timing.
