# Nafs Aql Seed

Nafs/Aql is the LifeOS vessel: the biological, psychological, and intellectual hardware that carries the rest of the system. It governs sleep, nutrition, fitness, emotional regulation, character refinement, learning, and cognitive clarity.

Execution quality depends on readiness. The system should protect sleep near seven hours or more, avoid workflows that harm the body or mind, and treat burnout, illness, and cognitive overload as operational risks.

The Aql layer uses deep reasoning, local embeddings, and careful agentic workflows to connect evidence across pillars without context drift.
